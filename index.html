Intercepting Outliers

We’re building a community for the most ambitious 14-18 year olds.

Apply by email

What we believe

Outgrown

The world is changing, and institutions aren’t. There are people that outgrow them faster than they can adapt, faster than anyone around them.

What we believe

14–18

Help has an exponentially greater impact the earlier it arrives, and between 14 and 18, it often doesn’t arrive at all. We aim to fix that.

What we believe

In person

Meeting people online is a start, but it rarely changes where someone ends up. An in-person house, with two weeks of building, eating, and breathing next to people who are better than you does.

Who we are

The next institution

We’ve decided to make the next institution for the people whose slopes are highest.

You get a room full of people just like you, each engrossed in their own personal project. We take care of the surroundings: flights, food, accommodation, so you can sink two weeks into something you truly believe in.

How it works

  1. Feb 2027

    Online cohort

    We’re starting with an online cohort at the beginning of 2027 to see who’s the best fit for our in-person one, where builders meet weekly over one month to build something they’re proud of.

  2. Weekly

    Mentors

    We provide you with compute credits. Experienced mentors will offer sessions to guide participants on a variety of subjects, and at the end of the cohort, you’ll be invited to show everyone what you’ve built.

  3. Summer 2027

    In person

    The 10–15 members who grew the most during the cohort spend two weeks living and building together, ending in a demo day. Free for participants.

Safety

All supervisors will be over 18. Every participant under 18 will have written parental consent, and the in-person sessions will be supervised at all times.

Who we’re looking for

Credentials do not matter.

Whether you’ve received an IMO Gold, got into ICML, or scored a 1600 on the SAT, we don’t take any of it into consideration.

If you’re obsessed with something, can explain why in a coherent way, are smart, agentic, and driven, you’re exactly who we’re looking for.

If you believe we can materially change your life outcome, you’re exactly who we’re looking for.

If you shut up and build, instead of looking for outside validation, you’re exactly who we’re looking for.
