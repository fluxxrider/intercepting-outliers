Intercepting Outliers

Manifesto

  1. 01

    The world moves faster than its institutions.

    Every year the world changes more than it did the year before. Schools, universities and programs change every few decades. Most people fit inside them anyway. Some outgrow them.

  2. 02

    Help matters most when it arrives early.

    The right mentor, room or conversation at 15 shapes everything after it. Between 14 and 18, that help rarely shows up. We aim to fix that.

  3. 03

    Slope over intercept.

    Where you start says little. How fast you’re climbing says a lot, and that’s what we look at.

  4. 04

    Credentials do not matter.

    Medals, scores and acceptances measure what someone else decided to test. We care whether you’re obsessed with something, can explain why, and keep building without waiting for outside validation.

  5. 05

    Rooms change people.

    Meeting people online is a start. Two weeks living and building next to people who are better than you is what changes where you end up.

  6. 06

    Money shouldn’t decide who gets in.

    The program is free for participants. We take care of flights, food and accommodation, so all you have to bring is your work.

  7. 07

    We’re building the next institution.

    It’s the place we wish had existed for us, built for the people whose slopes are highest.

If you’ve outgrown the institution that raised you, apply. We can intercept you early.
